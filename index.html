How I Work

AI doesn't replace
judgment. It sharpens it.

Research & Synthesis →

Field notes, interviews, observations — synthesised at a pace that used to take weeks.

AI clusters patterns. I decide which ones matter. Synthesis time cut ~40% at Allen.

Ideation & Exploration →

More divergence in less time — without losing the quality filter that makes ideas worth pursuing.

Prompt-driven divergence → rapid sketching → designer judgment owns what survives.

Prototyping & Iteration →

Figma AI, Lovable, and vibe coding for rapid UI — tested and refined before a sprint ends.

Speed to test. The human loop stays closed. Every shortcut is deliberate.

Systems & Edge Cases →

The unglamorous work that separates good design from production-ready design.

AI surfaces exhaustive states. I decide which ones the user actually encounters.
