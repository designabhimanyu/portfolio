judgment. It sharpens it.

About

I make complex things
feel simple.

My background is Visual Communication — which means before product design, I was an artist. College of Art in Delhi, then M.Des at NIFT. I've always believed that how something looks and how it feels are the same problem.

Wherever I am in the world, when I sit at my desk, I feel at home. I carry that same calm into everything I design — and I want the people who use what I build to feel it too.
