About

I make complex things
feel simple.

My background is Visual Communication — which means before product design, I was an artist. I studied at College of Art in Delhi, then did my M.Des at NIFT. I've always believed that how something looks and how something feels are the same problem.

Wherever I am in the world, when I sit at my desk, I feel at home. I carry that same calm into everything I design. I want the people who use what I build to feel that too.
